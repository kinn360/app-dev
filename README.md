## Overview

**FROM** is my favorite television series. It is a *science-fiction horror and mystery* series about people trapped in a strange town that they cannot escape.

> Every road leads back to the town.

The residents must work together to survive terrifying creatures that come out at night while trying to discover the truth behind the town.

## Why I Like It

I like **FROM** for the following reasons:

- It has a mysterious and unpredictable story.
- Every episode introduces new questions.
- The creatures make the series frightening and intense.
- The characters must make difficult decisions to survive.
- The town contains many unexplained places and symbols.

## What Makes the Series Interesting?

The most interesting part of the series is its central mystery. Anyone who enters the town becomes **trapped**, regardless of which road they take.

At night, dangerous creatures appear outside. The residents protect themselves by:

- Staying inside before dark
- Locking their doors and windows
- Using protective talismans
- Avoiding the creatures
- Working together to survive

## Favorite Part

My favorite part is watching the characters investigate the strange events happening around the town. The series includes mysterious elements such as:

- Strange symbols
- Underground tunnels
- Visions and hallucinations
- Mysterious children
- Unexplained voices
- Trees that can transport people

These mysteries make me want to continue watching and create my own theories about the town.

## Personal Review

I would rate **FROM** a **9/10** because its story is suspenseful, frightening, and full of mysteries. The series successfully combines horror with emotional character development.

However, the series often provides new questions before answering previous ones. This can sometimes be confusing, but it also keeps the story interesting.
